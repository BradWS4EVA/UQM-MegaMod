Darkness Falls Over Tristram

Desktop: Left-click to move / attack  ·  Right-click casts your skill
1–5 skills  ·  Q heal  ·  E mana  ·  I/Tab bag  ·  C character
Phone: Tap to move / tap a foe to attack  ·  tap a skill button to cast

Slay the bosses, descend the six levels, and destroy Diablo in the Inner Sanctum.
A self-contained HTML5 Canvas game · works on desktop & mobile · no install required
